# US Healthcare Data Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes US healthcare patient data using Microsoft Excel. The analysis focuses on understanding patient medical conditions, identifying factors that influence healthcare billing, and analyzing hospital admission and length-of-stay patterns.

The dataset contains 10,000 patient records with information related to patient demographics, medical conditions, admission details, insurance providers, billing amounts, medications, test results, and discharge information.

## Objectives

### 1. Analyze Patient Medical Conditions

* Analyze medical conditions across different age groups.
* Compare medical conditions between male and female patients.
* Identify prevalent diseases across age and gender segments.
* Identify the most common medical conditions among different patient groups.

### 2. Analyze Factors Affecting Patient Cost

* Compare average billing amounts across medical conditions.
* Analyze billing amounts by insurance provider.
* Compare billing amounts across admission types.
* Analyze billing amounts across different age groups.
* Identify the major factor influencing patient billing.

### 3. Analyze Hospital Admissions and Length of Stay

* Analyze monthly patient admission trends.
* Compare average length of stay across medical conditions.
* Identify conditions associated with longer hospital stays.

## Key Findings

* Medical condition is a stronger factor influencing patient billing compared with insurance provider, admission type, and age group.
* Cancer has the highest average billing amount among the analyzed medical conditions.
* Obesity has the lowest average billing amount among the analyzed conditions.
* Hypertension is highly prevalent among middle-aged and senior patient groups.
* Asthma is particularly prevalent among younger patients.
* Monthly admission volumes vary throughout the year.
* Diabetes shows the highest average length of hospital stay among the analyzed medical conditions.
